Accessibility Statement

Standards Target: WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance

1. Our Commitment

MFT Courier is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with motor, visual, auditory, or cognitive disabilities. We continually refine our user interface to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

2. Measures Taken

  • Semantic HTML5 structure across all layout containers, forms, and landmark regions.
  • Contrast ratios meeting or exceeding the minimum 4.5:1 ratio for standard text and 3:1 for large display text.
  • Visible focus indicators for keyboard navigation and screen reader tab order consistency.
  • Full support for prefers-reduced-motion media queries to disable unnecessary animations.
  • Direct alternative communication methods via telephone (+44 7501 419198) and email (info@mftcourier.co.uk).

3. Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need assistance completing a transport booking, please contact us at info@mftcourier.co.uk or call +44 7501 419198.
