Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 · Operating under UK GDPR

1. Introduction

MFT Courier ("we", "our", or "us"), operating from Greater Manchester (OL8), is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our customers, commercial partners, and website visitors in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data We Collect

To plan, execute, and invoice courier transport services, we collect necessary operational details including:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, telephone number, business entity name.
  • Logistics data: Collection postcodes, delivery addresses, recipient names, consignment descriptions.
  • Proof of Delivery (POD): Electronic signatures, delivery timestamps, photographic verification.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill transport contracts, calculate freight pricing, dispatch drivers, provide journey updates, and maintain accounting records required by UK law. We do not sell or lease personal data to marketing third parties.

4. Data Retention & Security

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Operational data is retained only for the duration required for legal, tax, and insurance purposes.
