Our Core Proposition: Moving What Matters Across the UK.

At MFT Courier, our purpose is simple, honest, and direct: we collect your goods from one location and transport them safely, securely, and efficiently to the destination.

Our Operational Philosophy

In an industry increasingly dominated by anonymous automated apps, delayed parcel hubs, and broken customer service trees, MFT Courier was built on a different model: direct, accountable transport.

When you book with MFT, you know exactly who is handling your load. Goods stay on the vehicle they are loaded into, avoiding the conveyor belt drops, multiple transhipments, and sorting risks inherent in traditional hub networks.

Based at Greater Manchester Hub

Headquartered in Greater Manchester (OL8), we sit at the logistical crossroads of northern commerce. With immediate arterial connections to the M60, M62, M6, and A1(M), we mobilise rapidly across the North West and reach any destination across England, Wales, and Scotland.
