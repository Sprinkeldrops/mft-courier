1. Scope of Agreement

2. Consignor Warranties & Packaging

The Consignor warrants that all goods are securely packaged and labelled to withstand normal road transit conditions. Dangerous goods (ADR regulated), hazardous chemicals, illegal contraband, and unmanifested hazardous items are strictly prohibited.

3. Liability & Transit Insurance

Consignments are transported under standard Goods in Transit policies. The Carrier's liability is defined by agreed policy thresholds unless an enhanced declaration of value has been pre-arranged in writing prior to collection.

4. Delivery Times & Delays

While MFT Courier exercises all professional diligence to achieve agreed transit windows, transit times may be subject to motorway closures, force majeure, extreme weather, and traffic incidents beyond the reasonable control of the driver.

5. Payment Terms

Commercial invoices are payable according to agreed credit terms (typically 30 days net from invoice date). Private and ad-hoc consignments require payment upon booking or delivery confirmation.
