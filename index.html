Direct highway dispatch from the heart of the North West.

Based at postcode OL8 in Greater Manchester, MFT Courier bridges local commerce with nationwide industry. Our operational location affords immediate access to the trans-Pennine M62 corridor and the north-south M6 artery.

05 · Operational Workflow

How a consignment moves with MFT.

A transparent 5-step operational protocol from the initial WhatsApp quote to physical proof of delivery.

STEP 01

BOOK

Message our dispatch desk via WhatsApp (07501 419198) with pickup and delivery postcodes, cargo details, and required timing.

STEP 02

DISPATCH

We allocate a dedicated driver and vehicle matching your exact size and weight specification without delay.

STEP 03

COLLECT

Driver arrives at your address, inspects load stability, secures the cargo with load straps, and verifies consignment details.

STEP 04

TRANSIT

Direct highway transport along primary UK motorway routes without intermediate depot halts or multi-package handling.

STEP 05

DELIVER

Direct handover to the recipient with electronic signature capture and immediate delivery confirmation sent to your team.
